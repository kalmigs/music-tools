Lock-screen audio spike

Throwaway page. It tests whether a binaural-beats session survives the iPhone lock screen. Delete it once the verdict is recorded.

  1. Tap one Start button. You should hear a soft tone pulsing once per second.
  2. Lock the phone. Wait about 90 seconds. Listen: does the pulse keep going?
  3. Unlock, tap Stop, then Dump log. A pass is the audio clock advancing about as far as the wall clock across the lock. A fail is any suspended or interrupted state, or the audio clock stalling.
  4. Repeat for each strategy. Clear the log between runs.
